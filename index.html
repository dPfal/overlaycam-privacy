OverlayCam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

OverlayCam doesn't collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

What the app uses

  • Camera: shows the live view and takes your photos. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Guide photos and places: kept only in the app's storage on your phone, together with the 3D map of the spot (ARKit), the phone's angle, your location and compass direction, so the app can guide you back to the same spot. Deleting a place deletes them.
  • Photos you pick: a photo you choose from your library as a guide photo is copied into the app's storage on your phone.
  • Saving to Photos: the photos you take are added to your photo library. The app can only add photos; it can't read your library. Location, tilt and direction are written into them unless you turn that off in the app's Settings. Photos in your library follow your own iCloud and sharing settings.
  • Sharing: a before and after image leaves the app only when you tap Share, and only to the place you pick.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Photos in your library stay until you delete them.
